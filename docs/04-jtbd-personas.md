# Jobs to be done and hypothesis personas

Personas are **hypotheses**, not research findings. No interviews were run.

## Personas

| Persona | Situation | Pain | What the Desk gives them |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Maya, RevOps manager** | Owns routing rules and the SQL dashboard | Cannot tell real improvement from mix shift; fixes ownership by hand | Versioned policy, refusals with reasons, denominators, regression case |
| **Leo, SDR** | Qualifies inbound demos | Handoffs bounce back without saying why | Missing fields listed per record |
| **Priya, AE (receiving owner)** | Gets 5–10 new SQLs a week across timezones | Context scattered across notes; SLA unclear | Source-cited brief draft, due time in her timezone |
| **Sam, VP Sales** | Reviews weekly conversion | Overall rate moves without explanation | Within-segment rates next to the overall rate |

## Jobs

- *When a batch of SQLs arrives, I want to know which ones are safe to hand off, so I don't assign the wrong person.* (Maya)
- *When my handoff is refused, I want the exact missing field, so I can fix it in one pass.* (Leo)
- *When I receive a lead, I want the pain, champion and timeline with sources, so my first touch is relevant.* (Priya)
- *When conversion rises, I want to know whether it rose within segments, so I don't reward a mix change.* (Sam)
- *When a write is retried, I want to be sure nothing happens twice, so the CRM stays clean.* (Maya)
