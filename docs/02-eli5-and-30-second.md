# ELI5 and 30-second explanation

## ELI5

When a salesperson finds a promising customer, they pass the customer to a colleague. Sometimes the same customer is written down twice, nobody is sure whose turn it is, the notes are old, or the pass happens too late. The Desk is a checklist that says "stop" when something is wrong, says exactly what is missing, and lets a person say "yes" before anything changes. Saying "yes" twice still only does it once.

## 30-second explanation

"Handoffs break in small ways: duplicate leads, unclear owners, stale qualification notes, late follow-up. And conversion reports can rise just because the lead mix changed. I built a Desk that imports a lead CSV, validates every row, deduplicates only by stable ID, routes by written territory and segment rules, and refuses anything ambiguous with the reason and missing fields. It exports a source-cited packet for a Dust agent to draft the brief. A person approves. The CRM update is idempotent, so a replay does nothing, and it's verified by a fresh read. Here it's all synthetic and simulated. The point is the operating discipline."
