# Role-fit and viability memo

**Role:** Revenue Operations at Dust (public listing). This is an independent concept by Ayo Ahmed, prepared as an audition. It is not affiliated with Dust and implies no employment or engagement.

## What the role asks for, and what the Desk shows

| Role theme (public listing) | Evidence in this build |
| --- | --- |
| Own lead routing and qualification | Versioned territory × segment policy; evidence gate with freshness |
| Handoffs between GTM teams | Packet → brief → approval → write → verify loop |
| Clean CRM data | Stable-ID dedupe, row validation, no merges by name, conflicts to people |
| Trusted reporting | Denominators everywhere; mix-adjusted cohort check |
| Operational leverage with Dust | A native agent drafts the brief from a strict contract; evals included |

## Viability

- **Cheap to try:** the policy and contracts are plain files; a shadow phase needs only a weekly CSV.
- **Safe by default:** nothing writes without a named approval; replays do nothing; ownership disputes stay with people.
- **Measurable:** SLA met rate per segment is countable in weeks. Conversion impact is not, and the plan says so.

## What I would want to learn first

The real SLA and who approves; where qualification evidence lives; how often owners are changed by hand today.
